## Surface

The Lab phone app, the one screen he uses all day: the conversation with his Mac's agent, live steps, approvals, shortcuts, the Mac at a glance, files both ways and search. Mode: Operate. Pixel 10 Pro Fold, outer 412 CSS px wide, inner about 852 by 884, inside an Android WebView, light and dark.

Chosen over four themed concept looks and a plain messaging look. The brief is bold fixed colour and real utility.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Pixel's own Material 3 Expressive at full volume, so the app feels native to the Fold and still has more colour and shape than anything else on the phone. Refuses the grey chat with one blue accent.

OWN-WORLD: Fixed violet primary owning the header, mint, sun and pink tonal containers, amber for needs-your-OK, green cookie for done, red clover for failed. Expressive shape library (cookie, burst, clover, flower, pill) morphing with state, 28 px card corners, Roboto Flex driven wide and heavy for display, Roboto Mono for commands. Spring motion.

STORY: He sees at once whether the Mac is up and how it's doing, sends or taps a shortcut, watches steps land, approves from one card, and finds any past request or file.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Tall violet header with The Lab in heavy wide type and three big stat shapes (battery burst, disk pill with a used bar, link cookie), the conversation in tonal cards below, a floating Review and Stop toolbar while working, a fat pill composer with coloured shortcut chips riding on it at the bottom.

FORM: Material 3 Expressive, my top-ranked grounded candidate (the pick card). Seed 4943adb0, re-roll 2.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Signature interaction

The header's stat shapes morph when state changes (charging to battery, online to offline), and the live step's loader cycles shapes. Spring timing, reduced motion holds still shapes.
