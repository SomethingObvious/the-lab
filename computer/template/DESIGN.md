# Design System: The Lab

Colours, type and corners above are custom properties in `app/app.css`. Colours keep their name with `--` in front (`colors.head` is `--head`), type roles become `--fs-*` and `--fw-*` (`typography.title-large` is `--fs-title-l` with `--fw-title`), and corners become `--r-*` (`rounded.card` is `--r-card`). Spacing is written as plain values from the scale. Change the property in `app.css` and update the value here in the same edit.

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Loudest Native App on the Phone"**

The page is Pixel's own Material 3 Expressive with the volume turned all the way up. It should feel like it shipped with the Fold, and still carry more colour and more shape than anything else on the phone. A tall violet header owns the top, pastel containers carry the Mac's numbers and the shortcuts, and every state the agent can be in has its own shape and colour, so he can read the screen from arm's length without reading a word.

It's a working tool he uses all day, one-handed on the outer screen. Density is moderate: big type and big targets, but no decorative space. Colour always means something. Violet is him and the app, green is work going on, amber is waiting on him, red is broken or stopping, and the four pastels are the Mac's figures and his shortcuts. The grey chat with one blue accent is the thing this refuses.

Motion is springy and short, and it's tied to state. Shapes morph when the state behind them changes, corners tighten under a finger, and nothing loops unless the Mac is actually busy or waiting on him.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Violet header block with The Lab in heavy, wide Roboto Flex and three stat shapes
- Tonal cards with 28 px corners, no borders, depth from tone rather than shadow
- One morphing shape library where each shape names a state
- Fat pill composer with colour-cycled shortcut chips riding on top
- Spring timing on shape and corner changes, still shapes under reduced motion
- Light and dark from one set of `light-dark()` tokens

## Colors

Saturated violet and four fixed pastels on a cool lilac paper, with green, amber and red kept for the agent's state.

### Primary
- **Header Violet** (`head`): the header block only. `head-deep` is its sunken layer for the search field and the expanded Mac details.
- **Lab Violet** (`primary`): his own messages, the send button, Allow, the primary sheet action and the notice button. It means "you" or "go".
- **Violet Container** (`primary-c`, text `on-primary-c`): quieter violet for the split button under his message, attached file chips, notices, the current request in the side list and text selection.
- **Link Violet** (`link`): links, text buttons, list markers, the "From you" and "From the Mac" tags.

### Secondary
- **Pink, Mint, Sun and Lilac** (`pink`, `mint`, `sun`, `lilac`, each with its `on-` ink): the same in both themes on purpose. They fill the header stats (sun battery, mint disk, pink link), cycle across the shortcut chips in that order with lilac fourth, and fill the attach menu and file icons. Sun is also the search highlight and the focus ring inside the header. Lilac replaces every stat colour while the Mac is offline.

### Tertiary
- **Work Green** (`work`, container `work-c`): the live run card and the working loaders.
- **Ask Amber** (`ask`, container `ask-c`, ink `ask-ink`): the approval card, a run card waiting on him, and the whole toolbar while it says Needs Your OK. `wait-mark` and `on-wait-mark` draw the waiting burst, dark brown with a sun icon in light and amber in dark, so it reads on amber containers too.
- **Done Green** (`done`): the done mark only.
- **Fail Red** (`fail`, container `fail-c`): Stop, failed marks, error lines under a step, the offline banner and its main button. `off-c` is the link stat's colour while offline.

### Neutral
- **Lilac Paper** (`bg`): the page behind everything, and the composer strip.
- **Surface** (`surface`): replies, the step list inside a run card, the message field, sheets, menus, Deny.
- **Surface 1 and 2** (`surface-1`, `surface-2`): the tray, closed run cards, pills, code blocks, day dividers. Surface 2 is a message still waiting to send and the disabled send button.
- **Ink, Dim, Faint** (`ink`, `dim`, `faint`): main text, secondary text, and times and placeholders.
- **Night Toolbar** (`toolbar`): the working toolbar, the snackbar and photo tile tags. It inverts per theme, dark in light and pale in dark.

### Named Rules
**The Paired Ink Rule.** Every fill has its own ink token (`mint` with `on-mint`, `head` with `on-head`). Changing a fill means checking its ink at 4.5:1 for text, and changing both if it falls short. "Make the header green" is a change to `head`, `head-deep` and maybe `on-head`, nothing else.

**The State Colour Rule.** Green, amber and red say what the agent is doing. Don't use them for decoration, and don't use the pastels or violet for a state.

**The One Token Rule.** Colours are written once as `light-dark(light, dark)` in `:root`. Never hard-code a colour in a rule, and never add a `prefers-color-scheme` block. `html[data-theme]` forces one theme for testing.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Roboto Flex (with Roboto, system-ui)
**Body Font:** Roboto Flex (with Roboto, system-ui)
**Label/Mono Font:** Roboto Mono (with ui-monospace, Menlo, Consolas)

**Character:** One variable family pushed hard. Display and titles run heavy and wide (weights up to 860, width up to 116%), body stays near regular, and Roboto Mono is only for what the Mac literally ran.

Both fonts are local `.woff2` files in `app/fonts/`. Roboto Flex is loaded with its full weight (100 to 1000) and width (25% to 151%) axes, so set `font-weight` and `font-stretch` freely within those.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (860, 36 px, 1, width 116%): The Lab in the header. Drops to the headline size when the header is compact.
- **Headline** (820, 24 px, width 110 to 112%): sheet and Files and Photos titles, and the big header stat numbers (narrowed to 92%, battery to 84% at 22 px).
- **Title Large** (750, 22 px, 1.25): the approval card's question.
- **Title Medium** (750, 16 px, width 104%): the toolbar's current step, Allow and Deny.
- **Title Small** (750, 14 px): section headings in Files.
- **Body Large** (450, 16 px, 1.45): messages, replies, steps, the message field. This is the root size.
- **Body Medium** (450, 14 px): run summaries, notices, the snackbar, answered lines.
- **Body Small** (550, 12 px): file sizes and photo captions.
- **Label Large, Medium, Small** (650, 14, 12 and 11 px): buttons and chips, times and day dividers, the link stat's second line.
- **Code** (Roboto Mono 400, 13 px, 1.5 to 1.55): raw commands, the approval's detail, error lines, code in replies.

### Named Rules
**The Role Token Rule.** Sizes and weights come from `--fs-*` and `--fw-*`. Reach for a role, not a number, and keep weights on the six steps (450, 550, 650, 750, 820, 860).

**The Tabular Numbers Rule.** Every changing number (battery, disk, times, durations) sets `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` so it doesn't jiggle as it counts.

## Layout

The page is one CSS grid filling the WebView: header, offline banner, the main area, and the composer, in that order. Only the main area scrolls. The header and composer never scroll away. The conversation is capped at 760 px wide and centred.

Spacing runs on 4 px steps with a few fixed habits: 3 px between the halves of a connected button group, 8 px between chips, cards and buttons, 12 to 14 px gutters, 18 px inside the big cards.

### Insets
The page draws behind the status bar, gesture bar and cutout. The header reaches under the top inset and pads its contents by `--lab-inset-top`. The left and right insets pad the whole grid. The composer's bottom padding is `--lab-inset-bottom` plus 10 px, and that variable is the keyboard's height while it's open, updated every frame. The sheet and the snackbar ride on the same variable. None of them have a transition on that padding.

### Folded (Under 720 px)
One column. The header is tall with the stats on their own row. While the agent works, the toolbar floats 12 px above the composer over the conversation, and the thread pads its bottom by the toolbar's measured height plus 32 px so the last message is never hidden under it.

### Compact Header
The header gives its height back (title at 24 px, stats at 48 px with their second lines dropped) when he scrolls more than 300 px up from the newest message, when search or Files is open, when the phone's text size is turned up, and when the screen is short. It comes back below 60 px from the bottom. The switch snaps, then the stats settle in, so no layout animates.

### Short Screen and Keyboard
When the height is under 560 px or `html` has `data-keyboard`, the app goes tight. The toolbar stops floating and docks in its own row above the composer, at 56 px with no shadow. On the outer screen the header also drops its stats, so the message box and the conversation keep the room. The offline banner still shows. Sideways under 500 px tall, while he types a message with the keyboard up, the header, the shortcut chips and the request list hide as well. A search keeps its field, and the message tray hides while it has focus so the results get the room.

### Large Text
If "Message your Mac" at 16 px draws more than 15% wider than it should, or the root font is over 18 px, `html` gets `big-text`. The battery and link stats become squares that grow with their text and the disk pill takes a row of its own, so nothing overflows sideways.

### Unfolded (720 px and Up)
Two columns. A 320 px requests list with its own search sits on the left, the conversation on the right. The header becomes one row with the title left and the stats right (at most 520 px). The header's search button is hidden because the side list has search. Files show four tiles across instead of three.

### Book
With `data-fold="book"` (or two horizontal viewport segments), the requests list ends at the hinge's left edge and the conversation starts at its right edge. The column gap is exactly the hinge. The header splits on the same line, with the stats right of the hinge. Sheets and the snackbar open right of the hinge.

### Tabletop
With `data-fold="tabletop"`, the conversation stands up in the top half and everything he taps lies in the bottom half. The composer row is the whole area below the hinge with its content pushed to the bottom, the toolbar is fixed 12 px below the hinge, and sheets are capped to fit below it. The snackbar sits just above the hinge. With the keyboard up, the toolbar docks at the bottom of the top half instead, the shortcut chips hide so the message box stays clear above the keyboard, the snackbar sits above the docked toolbar, and a sheet stands in the top half with all four corners rounded.

### Named Rules
**The Always There Rule.** The message box, Stop while working, Allow and Deny on every request, the offline banner and messages still waiting to send must stay on screen and usable in every one of these layouts, with the keyboard up, at large text, folded, unfolded, book and tabletop.

**The Hinge Rule.** Nothing he reads or taps crosses `--lab-hinge-*`. A new fixed or absolute element has to have a book and a tabletop answer.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is tonal. Cards are flat fills one or two steps off the page (`surface`, `surface-1`, `surface-2`, or a pastel), with no borders. Shadows are kept for the three things that actually float over the conversation: the working toolbar, the attach menu and the snackbar. Each mixes `--shade` so it stays visible in dark. Selection and the jump highlight are drawn as gap rings, a ring of `bg` and then a ring of colour.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Toolbar Float** (`box-shadow: 0 10px 28px -10px color-mix(in srgb, var(--shade) 55%, transparent), 0 2px 6px -2px color-mix(in srgb, var(--shade) 30%, transparent)`): the floating toolbar. Dropped when it docks.
- **Menu Float** (`box-shadow: 0 14px 34px -10px color-mix(in srgb, var(--shade) 45%, transparent)`): the attach menu.
- **Snackbar Float** (`box-shadow: 0 12px 30px -10px color-mix(in srgb, var(--shade) 50%, transparent)`): the snackbar.
- **Selected Ring** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px var(--bg), 0 0 0 6px var(--primary-c)`): his message while its actions are open.
- **Jump Flash** (`0 0 0 4px var(--bg), 0 0 0 8px var(--sun)`, fading over 1.6 s): the card he jumped to from search or Review.

### Named Rules
**The Only Floaters Rule.** A shadow means it floats over scrolling content. Cards in the conversation never get one.

## Shapes

Two kinds of shape. Rounded rectangles do the containing, and a small library of soft polar shapes does the signalling.

### Corners
Everything is rounded, nothing is square. Pills take half their height (20 px at 40 high, 24 at 48, 26 at 52, 28 at 56). Cards and bubbles use 28 px, the approval card and sheet 32 px, the header's bottom edge, the tray and the toolbar 36 px. Message bubbles keep one 8 px tail corner: bottom right for his, top left for the Mac's replies and photos. Grouped lists (search results, the file list, the shortcut editor) round only the group's outer corners to 24 or 28 px and keep 8 px between items. Connected button pairs (Deny and Allow, Review and Stop, the split button) round their outer ends fully and their inner ends to 6 or 10 px.

### State Shapes
Each shape is the same 96 point outline (`--sh-circle`, `--sh-cookie` and so on, generated at the top of `app.js`), so any one can morph into any other with a transition on `d`. Each shape means a state, wherever it appears.

- **Cookie** (9 soft lobes): done (green, with a check), the Mac online (pink link stat), and the send button when there's something to send, turned 40 degrees.
- **Burst** (12 points): waiting on him (the approval mark, a waiting run, the toolbar loader while asking), and the battery stat while charging or with no battery.
- **Clover** (4 lobes): failed (red, with an x), a failed step's dot, the offline banner mark, and the link stat while offline or unpaired.
- **Flower** (8 petals): the battery stat on battery, the link stat while connecting, and the default file icon.
- **Circle**: stopped, a plain finished step's dot, and the send button with nothing to send.
- **Pentagon**: working, held still, under reduced motion. Also one of the file icon shapes.
- While the Mac is working, the toolbar loader springs to the next of burst, cookie, pentagon and clover every 2 s as it spins. That cycle means working and nothing else. The working marks in the list and the steps hold a pentagon and spin, so only one shape is ever changing at a time.

File icons cycle flower, cookie, pentagon and clover down the Files list just for variety, which is the one place a shape is decoration.

### Motion
- **Spring** (`--spring`, a `linear()` curve that overshoots about 10% and settles): shape morphs (0.65 s), corner changes (0.35 to 0.5 s), entrances (`grow`, `rise`, `up`, 0.4 to 0.55 s).
- **Ease** (`--ease`, `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)`): colour changes (0.2 to 0.35 s), fades, and the 0.2 s exits.
- **Press**: every button tightens its corners while pressed (to 12, 14 or 16 px), and chips and bubbles also shrink a little (0.96 and 0.98).
- **Loops**: the working marks spin once every 3.2 s, the toolbar loader also steps to a new shape every 2 s, and the waiting burst turns once every 14 s. Nothing else loops.

Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, transitions keep only colour, background and opacity. Entrances become a 0.2 s fade, exits are instant, the loaders stop, working shows a still pentagon and waiting a still burst, and the jump flash steps on and off instead of fading.

### Named Rules
**The Shape Means State Rule.** Don't give a state's shape to something that isn't that state. A new state picks an unused shape or reuses its nearest meaning.

**The Morph Not Swap Rule.** Where a shape stays on screen through a state change, as the header stats, the toolbar's loader and the send button do, change its class (`s-cookie` to `s-clover`) on the same path so it morphs instead of replacing the element. Run cards, the request list and answered rows are redrawn when they change, so their new shape just appears.

## Components

### Buttons
Tactile and chunky. Every one is a pill that squares up under the finger.
- **Shape:** half-height pill, corners to 12 to 16 px while pressed.
- **Filled:** violet `primary` (Allow, the sheet's main action, the notice's action) or red `fail` (Stop, the banner's Open Tailscale).
- **Tonal:** `surface` on a coloured card (Deny, Try Again, the round attach button).
- **Text:** `link` colour with no fill (Copy under a raw command, sheet Cancel), still 48 px high.
- **Pills and split button:** 40 px high on `surface-1` or `primary-c`, with an invisible 48 px hit area. The split button's menu half turns its chevron while open.
- **Icon buttons:** 48 px, 24 px stroked SVG icon from the sprite at the top of `index.html`, pressed state `press` at 14 px corners.
- **Focus:** a 3 px `focus` ring 2 px out. Inside the header the ring is `sun`. Fields show an inset 2 px ring instead.

### Chips
- **Style:** 40 px pills on the tray, filled pink, mint, sun, lilac in turn by position, with an invisible 48 px hit area. The last chip (Edit) is `surface` with `dim` text.
- **State:** pressed tightens to 10 px and shrinks to 0.96. The row scrolls sideways and fades its right 36 px.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 28 px (32 px for the approval card).
- **Background:** `surface` for replies and files, `surface-1` for run cards, `primary-c` for notices, `fail-c` for the offline banner.
- **Shadow Strategy:** cards have no shadow (see Elevation & Depth).
- **Border:** none.
- **Internal Padding:** 14 to 18 px.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Message Field:** a `surface` pill in the `surface-1` tray, 52 px high and growing to 160 px, 26 px corners, `faint` placeholder.
- **Header Search:** a `head-deep` pill with `on-head` text and a `sun` caret and ring.
- **Sheet fields:** `surface-1`, 18 px corners, 48 px high.
- **Focus:** an inset 2 px `focus` ring, no outline.

### Navigation
There are no tabs. The header holds the title, Search (folded only) and Files and Photos. Search replaces the title row with a back button and the search field. Files and search results slide over the conversation and close with back. Unfolded, the left column lists every request with its state mark, and the current one is a `primary-c` pill.

### Header and Stats
The violet block with The Lab and three stat shapes: a sun battery shape (pink when 20% or lower), a mint disk pill with a used bar, and a pink link cookie. The shapes morph as state changes. Offline, the battery and disk go lilac and the link turns `off-c` clover. Tapping the stats opens a `head-deep` details panel.

### Run Card
The steps of one request. Closed it's a 48 px `surface-1` pill hugging its summary with a state mark. Live it turns `work-c`, and waiting on him it turns `ask-c`. Tapping a step shows the raw command in mono with a Copy button.

### Approval Card
`ask-c` card with a 56 px slowly turning burst and shield, the agent's sentence as Title Large, the raw detail in a `surface` mono block (capped at 180 px, 96 px when tight), and a connected Deny and Allow row 60 px high. Allow is half again as wide as Deny. The pressed one grows and its corners close to 16 px. Once answered it collapses to a one-line answered row with a small mark.

### Working Toolbar
A 72 px `toolbar` pill with a 52 px loader, the current step and "Working for 0:07", and Stop on the right. While a request waits on him the whole bar turns `ask` amber, the loader becomes a still burst on a dark disc, the label says Needs Your OK, and Review appears joined to Stop. ### Composer
The `bg` strip at the bottom holds a `surface-1` tray with 36 px corners: the shortcut chips on top, any attachments waiting to send, then a row with the round attach button, the message field and the send shape. His messages are `primary` bubbles on the right, at most 86% wide. Tapping one opens a split button, Save as Shortcut with a chevron that holds Send Again and Copy. ### Sheets and Snackbar
The sheet is `surface`, 32 px top corners, up to 640 px wide, with a 48 px drag handle that also closes it, over a `scrim`. The snackbar is a `toolbar` pill 8 px above the toolbar (or the composer), gone after 4.5 s, or 7 s for a failure.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep every control at least 48 px in both directions. Things drawn at 40 px get a `::before` that extends the hit area to 48.
- **Do** keep text at 4.5:1 on its fill in both themes. The shipped pairs run from 4.6:1 (`faint` on `surface-1`, light) to 14.8:1.
- **Do** call `lab.ready()` in the first animation frame after the first draw, as `app.js` does now. Never wait for the network, the history or fonts first. Past 8 seconds the phone shows its safe screen.
- **Do** pad anything pinned to the bottom with `--lab-inset-bottom` and let it move with the keyboard, without a transition of its own.
- **Do** give anything new a folded, unfolded, keyboard, book and tabletop answer before calling it done, and check both themes with `?theme=light` and `?theme=dark`.
- **Do** keep the Review and Stop pair, Allow and Deny, and the send button in the bottom half of the folded screen, where the thumb is.
- **Do** add icons as stroked 24 px symbols in the sprite in `index.html`.
- **Do** keep fonts, images and icons inside `app/`.

### Don't:
- **Don't** put the message box, Stop, or Allow and Deny anywhere they can scroll away, hide behind the keyboard or sit under the toolbar.
- **Don't** add an animation that runs while nothing is happening. The only loops are the working loaders and the slow waiting burst.
- **Don't** animate layout (height, padding, grid) on the header or composer. Snap the layout and animate shape, colour or opacity.
- **Don't** add shadows to cards, borders to containers, or square corners.
- **Don't** use green, amber or red outside the state they mean, or use a state's shape for something else.
- **Don't** reach for a grey chat with one blue accent. Restyle within the colour, shape and type roles above.
- **Don't** load anything from the internet. A CDN script or a Google Fonts link won't load on the phone.
